An independent studio, built for the long way of doing things.

Neurotech AI Solutions GmbH was founded in Berlin in 2025 to build AI‑powered products the slow, careful way: owned end to end, engineered for privacy, and designed to earn a permanent place on someone's home screen.

01

Why we exist

The interesting question isn't what AI can do. It's what it should do quietly on your behalf.

The last few years produced remarkable machine intelligence and remarkably little software that uses it with taste. Most AI products ask you to do more: more prompting, more checking, more managing the assistant. We think the opposite is the opportunity — software that takes real work off your mind and asks almost nothing in return.

That conviction shaped our first product, Purser: a chief of staff that works for you all day and interrupts you almost never.

02

Principles

  • Craft over speed

    We ship when the details are right. Vacuously green tests, silent failures, and "works on my machine" have cost this industry more trust than any missed deadline — our engineering culture is built on verifying with evidence.

  • Privacy is architecture

    We design so that misuse is structurally impossible, not merely forbidden: data minimization by construction, encrypted token custody, consent as an append-only ledger, deletion that provably propagates.

  • Independence

    We are founder-owned and build our own products on our own infrastructure. That lets us make decade-scale decisions about user trust without anyone else's quarterly incentives.

  • Berlin, deliberately

    We operate under German corporate law and the GDPR, and we consider that a feature. Software that handles a person's calendar, mail, and commitments should be built somewhere with real accountability.
