How the services fit together

Assess, design, build, enable, operate. Each service is a complete piece of work on its own, and each one hands over cleanly to the next.

Most clients enter through the readiness assessment or the AI strategy, because those answer the question that matters first: which workflows are worth automating, in what order, and what will it take. From there, the agentic workflow and agent development services deliver the first systems, and managed AI operations keeps them working.

Three services run alongside the rest: governance makes sure what you build can be approved and defended, training makes sure your people can use and own it, and data foundations removes the data blockers where they genuinely exist. A fractional Chief AI Officer ties everything together for companies that do not yet have an AI lead.

  • Entry pointsReadiness assessment, strategy, training
  • DeliveryAgentic workflows, custom agents, knowledge assistants
  • OngoingManaged AI operations, fractional CAIO
  • Always includedGovernance, measurement, knowledge transfer

Which service to start with

  1. 1

    You are not sure where AI would pay off

    Start with the readiness assessment. Two to three weeks, a scorecard, three recommended first use cases and a 90-day plan.

  2. 2

    You have ideas but no order, budget or architecture

    The AI strategy produces a ranked use-case portfolio, a target architecture, a governance model and a business case your board can decide on.

  3. 3

    You know the process and want it automated

    Go straight to agentic workflow automation or custom agent development. We validate the scope in the intro call and start with a six- to eight-week pilot.

  4. 4

    Your people search for information all day

    An enterprise knowledge assistant with permission-aware retrieval and citations is usually the fastest visible win.

  5. 5

    Legal, the works council or IT security are asking questions

    The governance service gives you an AI register, risk classification, policies and oversight design that answer them.

  6. 6

    Agents are live but nobody owns them

    Managed AI operations adds monitoring, evaluations and model updates. A fractional CAIO adds ownership at leadership level.

Frequently asked questions

Can we combine services or do them in a different order?

Yes. The services are modular. A common path is assessment → pilot → operations, skipping the full strategy until a second or third use case makes a roadmap worthwhile. We will tell you honestly if a service is not needed in your situation.

Do you implement, or only advise?

Both. The consultants who assess and design also build, together with your team or your existing IT partner. Everything we build is documented and handed over; you own the code and the deliverables.

What size of company are the services designed for?

From funded startups with twenty people to corporate business units with thousands. The formats scale: a Mittelstand assessment involves three interviews and one workshop; an enterprise assessment covers several functions and an architecture board. See our pages for startups, Mittelstand and enterprise.

Which platforms and models do you work with?

Whatever fits. Microsoft Copilot Studio and Azure AI Foundry, Google Gemini and Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, Salesforce Agentforce, SAP Joule, n8n, LangGraph and others on the platform side; models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral and open-weight families, including EU-hosted and on-premises deployment. We hold no reseller agreements.
