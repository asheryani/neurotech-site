Quick answers

What is an AI agent, in one paragraph?

An AI agent is software that uses a language model to pursue a goal across several steps: it reads context, decides what to do next, uses tools such as your CRM, ERP or email, and hands over to a person when a decision needs one. Unlike a chatbot, it completes tasks rather than answering questions. Our guide What is agentic AI? goes deeper.

How long until something is actually running?

Typically six to eight weeks from the start of a pilot to an agent working in production on a defined workflow, after a two- to three-week assessment. Pace depends mostly on system access and how quickly decisions are made on your side.

Do you work with companies outside Berlin?

Yes. We work remotely across Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the rest of Europe, and on site where it helps, particularly during assessments and workshops.

How do you charge?

In fixed-scope packages for assessments, strategy and pilots, and in monthly retainers for operations and fractional leadership. We quote after a short intro call, once we understand the scope. There are no licence resale margins in our pricing.
