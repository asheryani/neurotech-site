02

Focus areas

— Schwerpunkte
i.

Attention & time

Modern work drowns in notifications that all claim to be urgent. We build systems that decide what genuinely deserves attention right now — and stay silent about the rest. Silence is a feature, not a failure.

ii.

Privacy-first AI

Machine intelligence over personal data demands engineering discipline: data minimization by construction, on‑device processing where possible, server‑side privacy rendering, and GDPR compliance designed in from the first commit — not audited in later.

iii.

Native Apple craft

Deep platform integration is where software stops feeling like a website in a box: Live Activities, widgets, notifications that respect Focus modes, and interfaces built with Swift and SwiftUI to feel inevitable on the device in your hand.

04

How we work

— Arbeitsweise

  • Deterministic where it counts

    Decisions about your attention are made by an explainable, testable rules engine. AI assists with understanding content; it never gets to interrupt you on its own authority.

  • Restraint as a product value

    Every interruption must justify itself. Our software is engineered to interrupt less than it could, not as much as it may.

  • Owned end to end

    Product design, iOS engineering, backend infrastructure, and security engineering happen in‑house, in Berlin, under European data-protection law.
