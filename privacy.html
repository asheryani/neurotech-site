Privacy notice / Datenschutz

The short version: this website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does no tracking. Here is the long version.

1. Controller

Neurotech AI Solutions GmbH, Lückstraße 24, 10317 Berlin, Germany — represented by its Managing Director, Ashraf Al‑Eryani. Contact for all data‑protection matters: hello@neurotech‑ai.de.

2. What this website processes

neurotech‑ai.de is a static website. It sets no cookies, embeds no analytics or tracking of any kind, and contains no forms. We do not build profiles of visitors and receive no individual visitor data ourselves.

When you open the site, technically necessary connection data (in particular your IP address, requested URL, user agent, and timestamp) is processed in server logs by our hosting provider in order to deliver the pages and defend against abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in operating a functional, secure website.

3. Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub processes the connection data described above as our processor; transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, to which GitHub is certified. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Fonts

The site loads the Instrument typefaces from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.). When your browser fetches a font file, Google receives the technical request data (including your IP address). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. If you prefer to avoid this, content renders fully with system fonts when font loading is blocked.

5. Email contact

If you email us, we process your address and the content of your message to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Messages are kept only as long as the correspondence requires or commercial-law retention duties demand.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for Berlin, the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

7. Products

Our products (such as Purser, currently in development) will have their own privacy notices describing exactly what they process; nothing about product data is collected through this website.
