Purser. A proactive chief of staff for your working day.

Everything demands your attention; almost nothing deserves it right now. Purser exists to make that call for you — carefully, explainably, and one priority at a time.

01

The problem

Notification systems treat every app as equally entitled to interrupt you. None of them knows what matters.

A calendar alert, a newsletter, an expiring deadline, and a message from your most important customer all arrive the same way: a buzz, a banner, a badge. The judgment about what deserves attention — the actual work of a chief of staff — is left entirely to you, hundreds of times a day.

Purser takes that judgment on. It connects to the sources you choose, understands context, and surfaces the single thing most worth your attention — while actively keeping everything else quiet.

02

What it does

i.

Reads your context

Calendar, reminders, and — with your explicit consent — mail. Purser normalizes everything into one picture of your day, on a rolling window, with data minimization built in.

ii.

Ranks what matters

A deterministic attention engine scores urgency, importance, and confidence, and decides — via an explicit interruption ladder — whether something deserves the Lock Screen, the app, or silence.

iii.

One priority at a time

The current priority lives in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity with honest countdowns — never two at once, never stale claims.

iv.

Explains itself

Every recommendation answers "why am I seeing this?" with the actual factors that ranked it — and accepts corrections that change future behavior.

03

Engineering principles

  • Privacy modes on every surface

    Lock Screen and notification text renders in three user-chosen modes — Full, Discreet (the default), and Hidden. In Discreet and Hidden, sensitive names, subjects, and amounts never leave the server in the first place: the wire payload itself carries only category-level phrasing.

  • AI assists; rules decide

    Language models help understand content. The decision to interrupt you is made by a deterministic, versioned, unit-tested rules engine — the same input always produces the same decision, and every threshold is inspectable.

  • Least privilege, always

    Integrations request the minimum scopes that make a feature work, show you exactly why before you consent, and disconnect cleanly: tokens are destroyed immediately and imported data is deleted on a verifiable job.

  • Built under European law

    Purser is developed by a German GmbH under GDPR. Consent is recorded, deletion propagates end to end, and data protection is an architectural property, not a policy document.
